About

Breath first, sequence second

I found yoga when I needed it most. What started as healing turned into a 250-hour teacher training, and then a specialization in power vinyasa — flow built for strength, not just stretch.

I teach breath as the metronome and let the sequence follow it, not the other way around. Expect sun salutations built into longer arcs, arm balances broken into drills, and enough heat by the middle of class that the last ten minutes on the mat feel earned.
